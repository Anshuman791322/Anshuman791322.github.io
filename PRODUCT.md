# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, internship reviewers, collaborators, and peers who are evaluating Anshuman Singh as a student/fresher developer. They need to quickly understand his technical focus, project quality, and readiness for software internships or entry-level roles.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Anshuman as a practical Computer Science student building AI tools, machine-learning projects, computer-vision releases, and polished web interfaces. Success means visitors can quickly see what he builds, trust that the projects are real, and reach GitHub, LinkedIn, or email without friction.

## Brand Personality

Technical, focused, student-ready.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI portfolio templates, over-glowy sci-fi visuals, and corporate resume templates. The site should not feel like a copied landing-page layout or a fake senior-engineer brand.

## Design Principles

- Lead with honest technical work, not inflated claims.
- Make the student/fresher context clear without making the page feel junior.
- Use motion to clarify attention and add polish, not to hide weak content.
- Keep project evidence visible: names, stack, descriptions, repo links, and contact paths.
- Prefer sharp, readable interaction over decorative effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep all links keyboard-accessible, preserve visible focus states, and avoid motion that blocks reading or navigation.
